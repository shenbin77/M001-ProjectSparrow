## Full Description

### Welcome to 赤雀 — Where Every Hand Tells a Story

You inherit Crimson Sparrow, a once-proud mahjong club now drowning in debt. The tables are dusty, the old coach is gone, and three young players with raw talent and no direction are all you have left. The competition wants your space. The clock is ticking. Can you rebuild a mahjong dynasty from the ground up?

### What You Get

**Real Riichi Mahjong**
Every match follows real Riichi (立直) rules — draws, discards, calls (chi/pon/kan), riichi declarations, yaku and fu scoring, furiten, dora, and exhaustive draws. No fabricated rules. No probability hacks. The tiles play straight.

**Club Management RPG**
Scout new talent, train your roster, and shape their development. Each player has stats (reading, offense, defense, composure), a personality, and four skill slots you customize. Build a team that fits your play style — aggressive rushers, patient wall-builders, or psychological pressure artists.

**Career Progression**
Start in back-alley street matches and work your way up: city leagues, regional qualifiers, national tournaments, and eventually international competition. Every win earns reputation. Every loss teaches something.

**Characters, Not Cards**
Your roster aren't collectible cards with rarity tiers. They're people with names, backgrounds, quirks, and growth arcs. Recruit a shy prodigy. Clash with a veteran who thinks you're not ready. Watch a rookie develop into your ace. There is no gacha, no loot boxes, no pay-to-win.

**Skill System**
Train players to learn new skills or swap out old ones. Skills affect training efficiency and AI decision-making tendencies — not tile probability or legal moves. Experiment with different builds. Find what works.

**Multiple Clubs, Rival Factions**
You're not alone in this world. Rival clubs each have their own philosophy, talent pipeline, and competitive identity. Study your opponents. Exploit their tendencies. Build rivalries that matter.

**Save Anytime Between Matches**
Your career, your pace. Save after every victory, reload after a devastating loss, or play through both. Save/load works between matches — no mid-game save scumming.

### What This Is Not

- **Not multiplayer.** This is a single-player career game. All opponents are AI-controlled.
- **Not a gacha game.** No real-money currency, no loot boxes, no randomized purchases.
- **Not a mahjong client.** This is a career RPG that happens to feature real mahjong at its core.
- **Not final art.** Visuals are functional placeholders — geometric silhouettes and color blocks. The game is being judged on gameplay, not production art.

### Current State of Development

This is an early access-quality build. The core loop works: you can recruit, train, play matches with real Riichi rules, earn rewards, and progress through a small number of career tiers. Club management, skill customization, and save/load are functional. The feature set is intentionally focused for the first playable slice.

Features planned but not yet implemented include: expanded league systems, international tournaments, full character art, multiplayer, and additional regional mahjong variants.
